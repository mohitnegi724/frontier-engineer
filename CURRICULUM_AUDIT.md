# Curriculum and Watch audit

Reviewed 3 November 2026. Every lesson has 2–3 distinct explanations (49 videos total), ordered with an accessible starting point and another perspective or worked example. Stable lesson IDs and exercise contracts preserve Firebase progress, notes, and code drafts.

Selection used creator descriptions and visible YouTube search results, favoring established educators and widely watched explanations. Titles, publishers, and embedding availability for all selected videos were checked with YouTube oEmbed. View counts change and are not used as a permanent ranking. This is a curation and metadata audit, not a claim to have watched every second or a guarantee of comprehension or future playback.

## Scope and preservation

- Every video has a lesson-specific explanation of its connection to the same reading objective and challenge. Contract differences (language, index base, zero vectors, lexical retrieval versus generation) are made explicit.
- The StatQuest cosine similarity explanation remains the first vectors video, with codebasics as a practical second explanation.
- Introductory CS Dojo/Web Dev Simplified and visual WilliamFiset videos replace the longer MIT lectures for a gentler entry into complexity and BFS.
- The CSS auto-fit chapter is bounded at 18:52–22:31; the basic AbortController chapter is bounded at 1:15–2:19.
- Watch completion remains a lesson-level, self-reported learning-session checkbox. Adding alternatives does not reset existing completion. Video selection is remembered while the current account stays open, but is not completion tracking.
- Validation requires 2–4 distinct videos per lesson, correct topic ownership for every entry, primary YouTube source URLs, valid chapters, and no unfocused reuse across different lessons. Metadata alone cannot prove semantic accuracy.
